# SOP · Company items: issue, changes and hand-over

MyEden Group CMS V1.0 · **Items & Hand-over** (HR / Admin) and **My Items** (staff self-service)
Applies to every company item given to a person: company email and system accounts, keys and access cards,
thumb drives, laptops, phones / tablets, uniforms, name tags, the printed handbook, and any other item.

## Who does what
| Role | Does |
|---|---|
| HR / Admin (Items & Hand-over, edit rights) | Issues items, records every change and return, starts / edits the hand-over, approves it |
| Staff member (My Items switched on) | Confirms each item received; reports problems; confirms returns |
| Person they report to (or anyone above, or the approver set on the hand-over) | Approves the hand-over once everything is back |
| IT (for accounts) | Resets passwords; HR records *that* it was done, never the password |

## 1 · Issue an item (first day or any time)
1. Items & Hand-over › By person › click the person › **+ Issue item…**
2. Choose the item type, quantity, description (e.g. the email address, which key, size), tag / serial if any,
   date, condition, an optional photo. **Never type a password** — the CMS refuses text that looks like one.
3. The item shows as *Waiting for the person to confirm*.
4. If **My Items** is switched on for the person (Self-service Access), they get a bell reminder and tap
   **I received this** (optional phone photo). The confirmation is locked with the date and time.
5. Not confirmed after 3 days → HR bell reminder. Until My Items is switched on, HR keeps a signed paper
   acknowledgement and attaches its photo as a note on the item.

## 2 · During employment
From the person's window, choose an **Action** on the item:
| Action | Use when | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Returned | The item comes back (no longer needed) | Status *Returned*; the person confirms the return |
| Replace / renew with a new one | Worn uniform, broken device, new key | Old item closed (returned / lost / damaged), new item issued and confirmed again |
| Renewed (same item) | Same item, e.g. contract or licence renewed | Event on the item, condition updated |
| Move to another person | Key or device handed to a colleague | Closed for this person, issued to the other, who confirms it |
| Password changed / reset | Email / system account password changed | Date and who did it recorded — **not the password** |
| Lost / Damaged | Item lost or broken | Status recorded with what happened, optional deduction reference and photo |
| Write off | Item no longer exists / not worth recovering | Closed with the reason |
| Add a note | Anything else worth recording | Note (and photo) on the item's history |

Staff can **Report a problem** on any item (not received, wrong size, lost, damaged) — it appears in the item's history for HR.
Every step is kept in the item's **History** with who and when, and in the Audit Log.

## 3 · Offboarding: hand-over
1. **Opens by itself** when the leaving date is confirmed (last working day set in payroll or the Employee Master)
   for someone who still holds items. HR can also **Start hand-over…** from the person's window.
2. Deadline = the last working day (editable). Approver = the person they report to (editable; HR / Admin can always approve).
   The person's clearance becomes *pending*. HR adds notes if needed.
3. The staff member sees **Hand-over before you leave** in My Items with every item and the deadline.
4. Each item is received back: HR records **Returned** (date, condition, received by, photo); the staff member confirms.
   Accounts: IT disables / resets the account → HR records **Password changed / reset**, then **Returned**.
5. Items not recoverable are recorded as Lost / Damaged / Written off (with deduction reference for final pay if applicable).
6. When nothing is left with the person, the approver (in My Items) or HR / Admin clicks **Approve**: approver name
   and time are stored and the clearance becomes *cleared*. Approving with items still out needs a written reason.
7. Bell reminders: hand-overs due within 7 days, hand-overs ready to approve, hand-overs for the manager to approve.
8. Nobody approves their own hand-over.

## 4 · Records
- Item history: every issue, confirmation, change, return and note, with who, when and photos.
- Hand-over: deadline, approver, notes, approved by and when, items still out at approval.
- Audit Log: every action, by user.
